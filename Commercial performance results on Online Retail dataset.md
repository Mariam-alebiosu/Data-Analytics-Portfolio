## Commercial Interpretation

The analysis shows substantial geographic concentration, with the UK generating the large majority of completed revenue. Revenue strengthened materially during the later months of 2011, with November producing the highest full-month revenue and order volume in the analysed period.

Repeat purchasing is also significant: approximately 65.6% of identified completed-sale customers placed more than one completed order. At the same time, customer-level revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of high-value customers.

Cancellation activity is commercially meaningful, with 3,836 cancelled invoice identifiers and an estimated cancellation transaction value of approximately £896.8k.
